# Project Statement: CropCare

**Author:** Aayan Bhaisal | **Reg. No.:** 26BAI10444 | **VIT Bhopal University** | B.Tech AI & ML, 1st Year

## Problem statement

Small farmers and agriculture students often keep crop details on paper or in memory. When a crop shows symptoms such as spots, stripes or discoloured grains, they have no organised way to compare what they see with known diseases, and no record of which problems appeared on which crop before. Advice is often sought late and earlier cases are forgotten.

CropCare combines a simple crop record book with a symptom-based disease advisor and a history that can be summarised and exported.

## Scope

**In scope**
- Recording crops (crop, field name, area in acres, sowing date, season) with add, view, update and delete.
- A built-in list of 10 diseases for 5 crops (Wheat, Rice, Tomato, Potato, Maize), each with symptoms, treatment and prevention advice.
- Choosing symptoms from a numbered list and ranking the possible diseases with a set-based score.
- A saved history of advice, a summary report and CSV or text export.
- Input checking, error handling, a simple log file and tests.

**Out of scope**
- Machine learning or photo-based diagnosis.
- Weather, prices or soil analysis.
- Several user accounts, a web page or a mobile app.
- Insect pests and nutrient problems.
- Replacing an agriculture officer.

## Target users

- Small farmers who want a simple record and a first hint about a crop problem.
- Agriculture students who want a worked example of a rule-based advisor.
- Extension workers who want a quick reference and a log of the cases they handled.

## High-level features

1. Crop record management (Module A).
2. Symptom-based disease advisor with treatment and prevention advice (Module B).
3. History, summary report and CSV or text export (Module C).
4. Checks on every input, and damaged data lines are skipped instead of crashing the program.
5. Menu-driven command-line interface.
